# Netaji Sports Club: Design Direction (DESIGN.md)

This document establishes the authentic design direction, identity, and liveliness dials for the Netaji Sports Club exercise and gym equipment reference web app.

## Identity & Purpose
- **Brand / Product**: Netaji Sports Club (NSC) Exercise & Equipment Directory
- **Location**: Pandharpur, Maharashtra (Phone: 90 11 44 5000)
- **Primary Audience**: Gym members, personal training clients, and coaches working out on the gym floor who need immediate, reliable information on exercise form, matching machines, and target anatomy without lag or filler.
- **Tone**: Authentic, athletic, utilitarian, direct, and respectful. Zero fake hype, zero synthetic AI tropes.

## Liveliness Toolkit Dials
- **ENERGY**: **2 (Balanced)**
  - Punchy athletic volt lime accents against deep carbon and pitch obsidian, reflecting high-energy physical training without shouting or visual noise.
- **RHYTHM**: **2 (Consistent with Intentional Breaks)**
  - Clear structural modularity: quick filter pill grid, full machine directory cards with station badges, and expandable exercise cards with movement cues.
- **MOTION**: **1 (Calm & Functional)**
  - Snappy, responsive transitions (150ms to 200ms). No floating, bouncing, or parallax scroll animations. Motion exists strictly to indicate state changes (open/close cards, mode switch).

## Palette System (Strict 2-3 Core + 1 Deliberate Accent)
### Dark Theme (NSC Gym Floor Default)
- **Base Background**: `#0C0E0B` (Pitch matte obsidian)
- **Elevated Card / Surface**: `#141712` (Deep carbon)
- **Well / Header Border**: `#1C2018`
- **Primary Ink**: `#F5F7F2` (Crisp readable light slate)
- **Secondary Body**: `#C4CBBD` (Sage slate, WCAG AA compliant)
- **Muted Label**: `#858E80` (Clear neutral)
- **Deliberate Accent**: `#AFCB1E` (Official NSC Electric Volt Lime, used sparingly for active states, key toggles, and primary CTA)
- **Button Ink on Volt**: `#0C0E0B` (Yields a high-contrast 12.8:1 ratio)

### Light Theme (High Contrast Daytime)
- **Base Background**: `#F5F7F2` (Pearl linen)
- **Card Surface**: `#FFFFFF` (Pure white)
- **Primary Ink**: `#0F130D` (Deep forest ink)
- **Secondary Body**: `#2D3529` (Readable dark olive)
- **Muted Label**: `#5E6B57` (WCAG AA compliant)
- **Deliberate Accent**: `#577805` (Deep readable volt olive, 5.2:1 contrast against white)
- **Button Ink on Volt**: `#FFFFFF` (High contrast)

## Typography
- **Display Headings**: `Outfit`, sans-serif (Athletic, geometric, bold weight 700-800)
- **Body & Metadata**: `Plus Jakarta Sans`, sans-serif (Clean, exceptionally legible at small sizes in gym lighting)

## Principles & Anti-Slop Commitments
- **Evidence Over Claims**: Every machine listed physically exists at Netaji Sports Club Pandharpur. Every exercise corresponds to actual equipment.
- **No Em Dashes (R-02)**: Use standard commas, periods, or colons.
- **Zero Decorative Arrows (R-08)**: Button text describes the concrete action.
- **Matte Over Glow (R-13)**: Surfaces are crisp, matte, and tactile. Glow is eliminated.
- **Keyboard & Touch Parity (R-32)**: Every interactive card is accessible via Tab, Enter, Space, and Escape.
